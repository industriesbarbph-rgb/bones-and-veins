GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE DATAREAD ONLY
BONES & VEINS requests only the Google Search Console read-only scope when the operator explicitly chooses to connect Google. The browser receives a temporary Google access token for the active session. The static dashboard does not embed a Google client secret, does not place the Google access token in repository files, and does not use Google authorization to modify Search Console or any BarbPH product. Search Console metrics are used only to display SEO health information and to generate reports requested by the operator. The Google bay is kept separate from BONES & VEINS' own public-site health checks. BONES & VEINS does not sell Google user data or use Search Console data for advertising. Product cures remain approval-required. Google authorization is read-only and cannot approve, edit, publish, or deploy a product.
